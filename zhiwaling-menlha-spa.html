About

The Buddha of Medicine

The spa takes its name from Menlha, the Buddha of Medicine depicted in traditional Bhutanese iconography holding a bowl of healing herbs. In keeping with this lineage, treatments are centered around long-standing local healing customs rather than imported resort trends.

Therapies draw on native herbal compresses, warm botanical oils, and targeted bodywork designed to release deep tension, restore balance, and ease the body after active days outdoors.

“Treatments are centered around long-standing local healing customs.”Menlha · Medicine Buddha
The Art of Traditional Healing

Dedicated strictly to tradition

Menlha Spa is dedicated strictly to traditional bodywork and native herbal therapies. Every session is conducted in private rooms by experienced therapists, using warm botanical oils and traditional compresses to ease muscle strain and encourage rest.
